Literature Paper

I designed the first issue of the Yale School of Art literature paper, and set its concept. Cut paper forms in orange, blue and yellow crash through the grid, and the text sets itself into and around the shapes.

Each spread treats its subject as an object to pull apart: a gourd, a garlic house, a table of contents that refuses to sit still.
