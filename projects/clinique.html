Clinique

I designed the campaign and the online store for clinique iD, a hydrator built in two parts. A base takes a cartridge of concentrate. The product page sells it as an equation, base plus cartridge equals the bottle you end up with.

Each concern has its own colour and its own texture. Green is for irritation, blue for pores and uneven texture, purple for lines and wrinkles, orange for fatigue. A shopper can pick a base and a cartridge without reading a word.
