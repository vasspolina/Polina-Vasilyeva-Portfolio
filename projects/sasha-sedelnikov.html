Sasha Sedelnikov

I designed See you later, a photobook by Sasha Sedelnikov held in the Garage Museum of Contemporary Art in Moscow. I paced the monochrome sequences against the colour ones and against the paper, so the images set their own rhythm.

I designed the show identity for Пучки серебряного света (Bundles of Silver Light) at the Museum of the History of Yekaterinburg. The title runs vertically down the poster and each photograph takes the rest of the sheet.
