Sasha Sedelnikov

Publication design for See you later, a photobook by Sasha Sedelnikov, held in the Garage Museum of Contemporary Art, Moscow. Black-and-white and colour sequences are paced against the paper so the images set their own rhythm.

The show identity for Пучки серебряного света (Bundles of Silver Light) at the Museum of the History of Yekaterinburg runs the title vertically down the poster, letting each photograph take the rest of the sheet.
