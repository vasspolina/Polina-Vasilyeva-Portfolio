Lenka Ilic

I designed the identity and the website for Lenka Ilic Studio, a furniture practice in Miami and New York, and art directed the photography. The studio folds each object from recycled aluminium by hand in Delanson, New York. Each carries a debossed signature and a serial number.

The mark sits in one weight, small in the corner of the page and small on the metal, so the object is what you look at.
