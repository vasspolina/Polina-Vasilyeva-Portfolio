Sarah Crowner

Design of the complete monograph on the painter Sarah Crowner, whose sewn canvases and cut-and-stitched panels straddle the divide between fine and applied art. Cover, typography, grid, and the sequencing of essays and plates.

Susan Cross's essay Beetle in the Leaves runs tight to the margin against installation views and figures, while the paintings take full spreads. The layout borrows the logic the work is built on: panels butted edge to edge, colour meeting colour without a seam.
