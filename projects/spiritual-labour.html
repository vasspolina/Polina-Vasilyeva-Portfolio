SPIRITUAL LABOUR

A display face of capitals and figures, drawn as one weight. I cut the letters on a curve rather than a stem. Bowls swell and close, terminals turn back on themselves, and several capitals give up their counters.

Ninety two glyphs. There is no lowercase, so the page sets everything typed into it as capitals.
