Florida Blue

UI strategy and design with frog design for Florida Blue's member experience: Loom, an on-the-go companion that reads sentiment and triages urgency; Promise Card, a payment card that rewards health activity; and Health Hub, a member's healthcare in one view.

I led the user research and testing, set the core UX and visual principles, and delivered the design system and visual libraries behind it, tested for accessibility throughout. Concepts extend into CareBuddies peer support, rewards and discounts, provider profiles, and the marketing site.

The strategy sits underneath: each concept defined on its own terms, and the experience objectives mapped over time and scored, so the order of the roadmap can be argued for rather than asserted.
