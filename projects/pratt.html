Pratt

Website design for Pratt Shows, the institute's annual end-of-year exhibition: an index of every school and programme held together by a soft-edged mask that lets the work bleed through the interface.

That mask is the identity. It changes colour with the season, yellow, then white on grey, then grey on pink for Spring 2018, and crops whatever sits inside it, so a rotating banner frames a plaster sculpture one week and a field of cast forms the next.

Alongside it, video art direction for the show, print from the show postcards to the SCPS Fall 2019 brochure, and publication design for Prattfolio, the alumni magazine of Pratt Institute.

Pratt Institute — Adjunct Professor, 2017–2020 — Website, Video Art Direction & Publication
