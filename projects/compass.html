Compass

Marketing design for the real estate brokerage Compass: the wordmark set against its field of dashes, the For Sale card in both black and white, and a digital banner system sized for every placement.

A data-visualisation language for listings and market reports, covering call-out stats, inventory tables and summary figures, built to stay legible from a phone banner up to a printed sheet.

Per-listing collateral too: neighborhood points-of-interest maps that place a building among its restaurants, galleries, parks, gyms and transit, alongside the everyday pieces an agent hands over, business cards, announcement cards, an agent introduction, and the Compass and you brochure.
