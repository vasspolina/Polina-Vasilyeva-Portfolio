We tested cookie banners from 27 EU country lists.

About 1 in 5 cookie banners stayed open during the keyboard test.

The automated test tried to close banners using Tab and Enter.

Read how the test works.

The automated test used Tab and Enter on banners covering the page. A banner that stayed open may still respond to other keys or methods. This result does not establish whether a screen reader could access the website.

The tests ran in September and October 2026. Websites may appear in more than one country list, so these counts represent tests rather than unique websites.
