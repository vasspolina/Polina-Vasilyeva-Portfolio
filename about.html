Senior Product and Interaction Designer, New York / Madrid

I am a designer focused on UX/UI and Product Design, Digital Brand Experiences and Identity and Digital Accessibility.

At the moment, I'm living and working in New York as a Senior Product and Visual Designer. I hold a Master's degree in Design and Human Computer Interaction from Yale School of Art '17 and a Bachelor in Graphic Design from Gerrit Rietveld Academy, Amsterdam.

I had the opportunity to work as a Senior Designer and lead both junior brand and product designers at companies like Apple, Google, Optum and United Healthcare, Verizon, Frog Design, Wolff Olins and Apartamento Studios. My expertise is in design systems and experiences that are accessible, viable and tailored to business needs. I'm passionate about future oriented research and concept development in digital technology.
